MoviePalace Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: October 20, 2026

To request deletion of your data from MoviePalace or MoviePalace Poster:

  1. Email teammoviepalace@gmail.com with the subject "Data Deletion Request" and include your Telegram username or user ID; or
  2. Send a deletion request to the MoviePalace Telegram bot.

Requests are processed within 30 days, and you will receive confirmation once your data is deleted.
