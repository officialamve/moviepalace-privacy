Last updated: October 20, 2026

This policy covers the MoviePalace Telegram bot and the Instagram auto-posting app "MoviePalace Poster" operated by TeamMoviePalace.

Data we collect

  • Telegram: your Telegram user ID and username, used only to provide bot features (search, requests, settings).

How we use it

Data is used solely to operate the bot and publish posts. We do not sell, rent or share personal data with third parties for advertising.

Data is kept only as long as needed to provide the service, and is deleted on request or when no longer needed.
